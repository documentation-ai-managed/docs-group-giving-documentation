## Creating Your First Wishlist

Wishlists are the core of Group.Giving, allowing group members to suggest and coordinate gift items without spoilers. Each wishlist can be shared privately within your group, ensuring everyone knows what to get.

  <Step title="Navigate to Wishlists" icon="arrow-right">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New List" icon="plus">
    Click 'New Wishlist' and enter details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Items" icon="package">
    Add items with descriptions and links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with Group" icon="share-2">
