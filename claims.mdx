## Understanding Gift Claims

Gift claiming is the secret weapon of Group.Giving, allowing participants to reserve items anonymously. This prevents duplicates and maintains the surprise element of group gifting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="View Available Items" icon="eye">
    Browse unclaimed items on group wishlists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim Item" icon="check-circle">
    Click 'Claim' on your chosen item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Purchase" icon="credit-card">
    Purchase and mark as completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver Gift" icon="gift">
    Coordinate delivery with the recipient.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What happens when someone else claims first?" default-open="true">
    You'll receive a notification and can choose another item.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I unclaim an item?" default-open="false">
    Yes, within 24 hours of claiming, to allow flexibility.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
